Du an t5b1 - STM32F103C8T6, FreeRTOS, 3 kenh PWM TIM2 va 3 nut nhan.
Chan LED: PA0/TIM2_CH1, PA1/TIM2_CH2, PA2/TIM2_CH3.
Nut active-low voi pull-up noi: PB12 SELECT, PB13 UP, PB14 DOWN.
Build: make
Nap ST-LINK: make flash
Luu y: mac dinh clock ngoai HSE 8 MHz va PLL x9 = 72 MHz.
Kiem tra thach anh tren board truoc khi nap. Dung dien tro han dong cho moi LED.
